Safety Test Prep

What you can pay for

Study help: practice questions, flashcards, and keeping the labs online. Cash App or crypto. Optional. Not fundraising. Not a 501(c)(3). Not tax-deductible.

Safety Test Prep provides independent educational practice materials and is not affiliated with OSHA, the U.S. Department of Labor, any DMV, the American Heart Association, or the American Red Cross. Practice content does not provide certification or an OSHA Outreach completion card.

What you cannot pay for here

An OSHA 10 or 30 card. A California CDL. An AHA or Red Cross CPR card. A guaranteed pass. Skipping the official class, DMV, or skills session. If an ad says we “get you certified,” that ad is wrong.

A 10/30 card requires an authorized Outreach trainer. A CDL is issued by the DMV. A CPR card requires an authorized instructor and skills check.
