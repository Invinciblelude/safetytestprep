Common questions

Are these official OSHA, DMV, or CPR tests?

No. These are original independent practice quizzes. Safety Test Prep does not issue OSHA cards, CDL licenses, or CPR/AED certifications.

Are the practice tests free?

Fall protection, California CDL General Knowledge, and Adult CPR are free. The full OSHA, CDL, and CPR banks are $9.99 for 30 days on this device. Payment does not buy a card or license.

Where should I start?

Use the Fall Protection quiz for OSHA-topic study, California CDL General Knowledge for permit prep, or Adult CPR before a skills class. Roofers, laborers, electricians, and HVAC apprentices can start on the trade pages.

Can employers or trade schools use this?

Yes. Send a trade page or quiz link to a crew or class. It is unpaid study, not a substitute for required employer training or an Outreach card. For teams has the links to forward.
