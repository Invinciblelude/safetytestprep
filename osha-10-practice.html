Is this an official OSHA 10 test?

No. Original practice only. A 10- or 30-hour card comes from an authorized Outreach trainer after the required hours.

OSHA does not publish one official exam. Trainers write their own tests. This site’s 10-style mock is 25 questions (18 to pass) and the 30-style mock is 50 questions (35 to pass). See OSHA 10 vs 30.
