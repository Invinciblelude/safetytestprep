About

Independent practice. Not a training provider.

Safety Test Prep is an independent learning platform built for workers, job seekers, tradespeople, drivers, and safety-minded teams.

Our purpose

Make practical workplace-safety knowledge easier to understand and easier to practice. We publish original study quizzes, checklists, and plain-language practice covering common topics in construction, transportation, workplace safety, emergency response, and career readiness.

Practice safety knowledge. Build workplace confidence. Get job-ready.

What this site is — and is not

Our resources are designed to supplement—not replace—employer training, hands-on instruction, official course providers, licensing agencies, or professional medical guidance. Requirements vary by employer, industry, location, and credential.

We do not issue OSHA Outreach completion cards, CDL licenses, CPR/AED certifications, or government credentials. Why this site exists (the short story).
