Study help · not a card

How this OSHA lab is allowed to exist

A 10- or 30-hour card can only be issued by an authorized OSHA Outreach trainer after the required hours. This site is practice only.

What you can tell people (and Google)

Free OSHA 10 and OSHA 30 practice tests. Original questions modeled on 29 CFR 1910 and 1926. Not official DOL items. Use it to study before a real class.

What you cannot say

Do not say this is an OSHA certification, an official exam, or that paying here gets a card in the mail. Do not use the OSHA logo as if you were an Outreach trainer.

How you get paid

The quizzes stay free. After a mock, a $5 / $9 / $19 tip is optional via Cash App or crypto (not PayPal). That is pay for keeping the lab online — not a donation, not a credential. Paste your $cashtag and wallet in js/support.js.
