Why this site exists

People about to sit an OSHA class, a California CDL permit, or a CPR written test need practice they can use tonight — without buying a stolen “official exam dump” and without thinking a website can issue the card.

Who it’s for

Apprentices and helpers studying OSHA 10 or 30 before a real Outreach course. Permit students grinding General Knowledge, Air Brakes, and Combination before the DMV. Anyone reviewing CPR/AED before a skills class. If you need the credential, you still go to an authorized trainer, the DMV, or an AHA/Red Cross instructor. This lab is the homework.

Why I built it

I’m Vince, in Sacramento. I wrote original practice banks so the quizzes are free and honest — modeled on the standards and the handbook, not copied from a locked exam. I keep them online. I do not run a training center and I do not pass you.
