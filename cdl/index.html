Commercial-driver knowledge-test prep

Required written tests: General Knowledge (50), Air Brakes (25), Combination Vehicles (20). Pass each at 80% at the DMV. The banks are larger than the real tests so each attempt draws a new mix. Optional endorsements are in the 7-category mock. This is study help, not the DMV. Last reviewed August 31, 2026. How this lab is allowed to exist · See an error? Tell us.

Official source: California Commercial Driver Handbook.

Best free study order

  1. Read California CDL handbook sections for General Knowledge, Air Brakes, and Combination Vehicles.
  2. Take the subject tests here until you consistently score 90% or higher.
  3. Write down every missed topic, not just the missed question.
  4. Drill the 50-question mock and the flashcards before permit day.
