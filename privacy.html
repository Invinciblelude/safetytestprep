Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026. We will note material changes on this page with a new effective date.

Who we are

Safety Test Prep is an independent educational website at safetytestprep.com. Contact: hello@safetytestprep.com.

Information we collect

We do not require an account. We do not run an email list or a contact form on this site.

Quiz progress on your device. Flashcard “known” marks are stored in your browser’s local storage. That data stays on your device unless you clear it. We do not receive a copy.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and timestamps as described in GitHub’s own privacy policy. We do not operate a separate analytics product on these pages.

Payments. Optional study-support payments go through Cash App or a crypto network you choose. Those companies (or networks) process the payment. We do not collect card numbers on this site.

Email you send us. If you email us, we receive whatever you include in the message so we can reply.

How we use information

Device storage is used only to remember flashcard progress. Email is used only to respond to you. We do not sell personal information. We do not use the site to serve third-party advertising.

Who we share with

We do not sell or rent visitor lists. Hosting is provided by GitHub. If you pay, Cash App or the crypto network you use is a third party. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices (including California)

You can clear local storage in your browser to remove flashcard marks. You can stop using the site at any time. To review or request changes to personal information you emailed us, write to the address above. We will announce policy changes by updating this page and the effective date.

Children

This site is intended for adults preparing for workplace, CDL, or skills-class study. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
