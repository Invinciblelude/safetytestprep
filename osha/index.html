Best study order

  1. Learn worker rights and employer duties (Introduction to OSHA), then the construction Focus Four.
  2. Drill Fall Protection, Excavation, Electrical, and PPE until you consistently score 80% or higher.
  3. Add LOTO, HazCom, health hazards, and confined spaces for OSHA 30 depth.
  4. Take the 25- and 50-question mocks, then review every missed item in the question bank.

Official sources: OSHA Outreach Training, 29 CFR 1926 (Construction), and 29 CFR 1910 (General Industry).
