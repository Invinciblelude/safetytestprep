About Safety Test Prep

I’m Vince.

I created Safety Test Prep to share practical study support based on real experience across jobsites, restaurant operations, driving, and emergency response.

The goal is simple: help workers, job seekers, and career changers review safety fundamentals in plain language and practice before formal training, testing, or a new role.

Safety Test Prep provides independent educational practice materials only. I do not issue OSHA cards, CDL licenses, CPR/AED certifications, medical credentials, or government-approved certificates.
